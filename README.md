# AI Content Distribution Engine for n8n

A powerful n8n workflow that automatically repurposes blog posts and articles into multi-channel social media content using AI and distributes them across your target platforms.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **Automated AI Repurposing**: Converts long-form blog posts into engaging short posts for social channels.
- **Multi-Platform Ready**: Designed to feed content directly to social media pipelines and distribution channels.
- **Google Sheets Management**: Centralizes your published links, content status, and scheduled distribution queue.
- **Zero Manual Copy-Paste**: Saves hours by automating cross-platform content marketing.

## 🛠️ How It Works

1. **Trigger**: Pulls newly published articles or scheduled content from Google Sheets.
2. **AI Transformation**: Generates tailored social posts, captions, and key takeaways via OpenAI.
3. **Distribution Execution**: Automatically pushes content to target webhooks / social platforms.
4. **Status Sync**: Updates publication records and links directly back into Google Sheets.
